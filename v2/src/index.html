Neural Entrainment Frequency Reference

Binaural Beat Mechanism

Binaural beats generate auditory interference patterns by presenting two pure tones with slight frequency differences to each ear. The brain processes this difference as a beat frequency, potentially inducing corresponding brainwave entrainment patterns.

Solfeggio Frequency Set

Solfeggio frequencies are specific tones studied for their potential neuroacoustic effects on cognitive and physiological processes. Research into these frequencies examines their impact on brain activity and autonomic nervous system responses.

Frequency-Response Mapping

Different frequencies target specific neural oscillation patterns and physiological responses. These empirically-studied frequencies are categorized by their measured effects on cognitive performance:

  • Cognitive Enhancement: Beta (14-30Hz) and Gamma (30-100Hz) frequencies increase attention span and working memory performance
  • Creative Problem-Solving: Theta (4-8Hz) frequencies correlate with increased divergent thinking and insight problem-solving
  • Sleep Induction: Delta (0.5-4Hz) frequencies promote slow-wave sleep and reduce sleep onset latency
  • Relaxed Focus: Alpha (8-14Hz) frequencies maintain alertness while reducing cortisol levels
  • Physiological Regulation: Specialized frequencies target autonomic nervous system regulation and stress response modulation

Getting the Best Results

  1. Use quality headphones for binaural beats to work effectively
  2. Start with shorter sessions (15-30 minutes) and observe how you respond
  3. Choose frequencies based on your current needs and desired state
  4. Keep volume at a comfortable level - louder isn't better
  5. Create favorites lists for different activities using the pin feature

Important Notes

  • Always use comfortable volume levels
  • If you experience any discomfort, stop using immediately
  • This is not a substitute for medical treatment
  • Some frequencies may be below human hearing range but can still have effects
